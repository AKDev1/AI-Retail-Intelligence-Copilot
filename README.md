# AI Retail Intelligence Copilot

An AI-powered decision-support system designed for small ecommerce sellers, D2C founders, and marketplace vendors who need actionable business intelligence without enterprise-level complexity.

## What It Does

The AI Retail Intelligence Copilot transforms sales data into clear, explainable business recommendations through:

- **Conversational AI Interface** – Ask business questions in plain language and get actionable insights
- **Demand Forecasting** – Predict future sales with confidence intervals and transparent explanations
- **Pricing Intelligence** – Get optimal pricing recommendations based on demand patterns
- **Inventory Risk Detection** – Receive alerts for potential stockouts and overstock situations
- **Market Trend Analysis** – Discover emerging product opportunities
- **Visual Dashboard** – See key metrics, trends, and priority actions at a glance

## Key Features

✨ **Explainable AI** – Every recommendation includes clear reasoning, confidence levels, and data limitations  
🎯 **No Technical Expertise Required** – Simple language and intuitive interface  
🧩 **Modular Architecture** – Independent services for pricing, forecasting, inventory, and trends  
🔒 **Secure Data Handling** – Session-based data management with encryption  
🚀 **Hackathon Prototype** – Uses synthetic datasets and lightweight ML models for demonstration

## Target Users

Small ecommerce sellers, online marketplace vendors, and retail business owners who struggle with data analysis and need a simple AI assistant to convert data into clear decisions.

## Quick Start

1. Upload your sales data (CSV format) or generate synthetic demo data
2. View the dashboard for instant insights on revenue, trends, and alerts
3. Ask the AI Copilot questions like:
   - "Which products should I reorder soon?"
   - "Should I change my prices?"
   - "What are my best sellers?"
   - "What products should I add to my catalog?"

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Web UI with dashboard and chat interface
- **Backend**: REST API with modular service architecture
- **AI Services**: Demand forecasting, pricing optimization, inventory monitoring, trend analysis
- **Data Processing**: CSV ingestion with validation and quality reporting

## Hackathon Scope

This is a prototype demonstration focusing on:
- Explainable AI outputs with transparent limitations
- Simplified ML models (time series analysis, elasticity estimation)
- Synthetic datasets for demonstration purposes
- Modular architecture designed for incremental enhancement
